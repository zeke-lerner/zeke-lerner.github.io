Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Hermes Agent is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

The app accesses only the owner's own Google account, and only with the owner's consent:

  • Gmail, with read-only access.
  • Google Calendar.

How data is handled

  • Data stays on the owner's computer.
  • Nothing is shared with, transferred to, or sold to anyone.
  • Nothing is used for advertising.
  • No data from other users is collected, because there are no other users.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
